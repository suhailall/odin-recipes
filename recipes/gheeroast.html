Ghee Roast (Neyy Roast)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups dosa batter
  • 3–4 teaspoons ghee (neyy), or more to taste
  • A little water, if the batter is too thick
  • Salt, only if needed

Step-by-step preparation

  1. Stir the dosa batter well. It should be pourable but not watery. Add a little water if it is too thick.

  2. Heat a flat dosa pan over medium heat. If it's extremely hot, sprinkle a few drops of water; they should sizzle and evaporate. Wipe the surface lightly before adding the batter.

  3. Pour one ladle of batter into the centre. Immediately spread it in wide, circular motions from the centre outward. Make it as thin as possible without creating holes.

  4. Drizzle about 1 teaspoon of ghee over the dosa and around its edges. Let it cook on medium to medium-low heat until golden brown and crisp, usually around 3–5 minutes depending on the pan.

  5. Once the dosa is crisp, roll it into a long cylinder or fold it into a triangle. You usually don't need to flip it. Serve immediately with coconut chutney and sambar.
