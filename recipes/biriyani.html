Biriyani

Ingredients

For the Rice

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 4-5 cups water, for boiling
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 3 cardamom pods
  • 3 cloves
  • 1 small cinnamon stick
  • salt to taste
  • 1 tsp ghee or oil

For the chicken masala

  • 500 g chicken, preferably bone-in
  • 2 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies, slit
  • ½ cup plain yogurt (curd)
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 2 tsp biriyani masala
  • ½ tsp garam masala (optional)
  • ¼ cup chopped coriander leaves
  • ¼ cup chopped mint leaves
  • 3 tbsp oil or ghee
  • Salt to taste
  • Juice of ½ lemon

Optional garnishes

  • Fried onions
  • Cashews and raisins
  • A little saffron soaked in warm milk

Step-by-step preparation

  1. Wash the basmati rice 2–3 times and soak it for 20 minutes. Drain the water before cooking.

  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large, heavy-bottomed pot. Add the sliced onions and fry until golden brown. You can keep a little aside for garnish.

  3. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chillies. Fry for a minute. Add tomatoes, turmeric, chilli powder and biriyani masala. Add the chicken, yogurt, salt, coriander and mint. Mix well, cover and cook for about 20–25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is cooked through. Add a splash of water if the masala becomes too dry.

  4. Bring 4–5 cups of water to a boil. Add salt, bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and 1 tsp ghee. Add the drained rice and cook until it is about 80% cooked—the grains should be soft on the outside but still slightly firm in the middle. Drain immediately.

  5. Spread the cooked chicken masala evenly in the pot. Add the rice on top. Sprinkle the reserved fried onions, coriander, mint, lemon juice and optional saffron milk.

  6. Cover the pot tightly. Cook on very low heat for 15–20 minutes. If your pot is thin, place a flat pan underneath to prevent the bottom from burning. Turn off the heat and let it rest for 10 minutes. Gently mix the rice and chicken before serving.
