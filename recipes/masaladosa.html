Masala Dosa

Ingredients

For the dosa

  • 2 cups ready-made dosa batter
  • Oil or ghee, as needed
  • Water, if needed to adjust the batter consistency

For the potato masala

  • 3 medium potatoes
  • 2 medium onions, thinly sliced
  • 2 green chillies, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger, finely chopped
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ cup water
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves (optional)
  • ½ tsp urad dal (optional)

Step-by-step preparation

  1. Boil the potatoes until soft, about 15–20 minutes depending on their size. Peel them and mash roughly, leaving a few small chunks for texture.

  2. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pan. Add mustard seeds and let them splutter. Add optional urad dal and fry until lightly golden. Add curry leaves, ginger and green chillies.

  3. Add sliced onions and fry until soft. Add turmeric and salt, then mix well. Add the mashed potatoes and ¼ cup water. Cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring until everything combines into a soft, moist filling. Finish with coriander leaves if desired.

  4. Heat a flat dosa tawa or non-stick pan over medium heat. Pour a ladleful of batter into the centre and spread it in circular motions to make a thin dosa. Drizzle a little oil or ghee around the edges.

  5. Cook for about 2–4 minutes until the bottom becomes golden and crisp. Spread 2–3 tablespoons of potato masala in the centre.

  6. Fold the dosa over the filling or roll it into a cylinder. Serve hot with coconut chutney and sambar.
